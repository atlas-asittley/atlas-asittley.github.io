The Routine

Do the mobility daily-ish, the glute-med work as your leg-day warm-up, the core 2–3×/week. Log what you did and hit save at the bottom. Everything stays OUT of the groin pinch.

Avoid

  • Deep squats / "sit between the hips" — cap depth above the pinch, upright, toes out
  • Loaded forward bends / conventional or deep RDL — use trap-bar / hip thrust
  • Aggressive stretching into deep hip flexion (pigeon, deep squat holds)
  • Loaded end-range hip flexion + adduction + internal rotation (the pinch position)

Not medical advice — training/mobility we're self-managing. Also worth doing: an updated MRI (5+ yr old, symptoms persist). New/worsening/radiating pain → see a doctor, don't push through.

How To Run It fold in — no separate day

  • Daily ~5 min: mobility (90/90 + airplanes) — responds to frequency, not intensity
  • Every leg day, as WARM-UP (before the bar): glute-med activation — pre-fires the right muscles so the QL doesn't compensate
  • 2–3×/week: anti-QL core carries + single-leg work (post-lift or filler)
  • On the bar: depth cap + hip-friendly swaps (see Workout / program)

Give it a genuine 6–8 weeks. Feels pointless at first (low load), then ~week 3–4 the back usually quiets during squats — that's the compensation unwinding.

Daily Mobility ~5 min, most days

Aimed out of the pinch.

  1. 90/90 hip switches — 8–10/side, slow. Add gentle back-knee lift-offs for active internal rotation
  2. Hip airplanes (supported) — 5/side, slow. IR control under load
  3. Couch stretch / hip flexor — keep it (it helps you); ease off if it ever refers into the groin pinch

Glute-Med Activation leg-day warm-up, before the bar

Progress in order as each gets easy.

  1. Side-lying hip abduction — 2–3 × 12–15, slow. Top leg slightly behind the body (straight up recruits the TFL — the thing we're quieting)
  2. Banded lateral walks — band above knees, low stance, no torso sway. 2–3 × 10 steps each way
  3. Step-downs — on a box, slowly lower the other heel to tap the floor, drive back up; standing knee must not cave in. Key single-leg builder. 3 × 8/side

Anti-QL Core + Single-Leg 2–3×/week

  • Suitcase carry — one heavy DB, walk tall, don't let it pull you sideways. Load the left hand to force the right side to stabilize. 3 × 30–40 steps
  • Side plank — knees if needed, build to full. 2–3 × 20–40 s/side (right priority)
  • Pallof press — band/cable anti-rotation, resist the twist. 2–3 × 10/side
  • Single-leg RDL — light, neutral spine, hip stability + posterior chain. 3 × 8/side

Avoid

  • Deep squats / "sit between the hips" — cap depth above the pinch, upright, toes out
  • Loaded forward bends / conventional or deep RDL — use trap-bar / hip thrust
  • Aggressive stretching into deep hip flexion (pigeon, deep squat holds)
  • Loaded end-range hip flexion + adduction + internal rotation (the pinch position)

Not medical advice — training/mobility we're self-managing. New, worsening, or radiating pain → see a doctor, don't push through.

How to take your measurements

Consistency beats precision — the same spot, same way, every time is what makes the trend real. Wrap the tape where the green band sits.

Ground rules — do these every time

  • 1Morning, fasted, minimal clothing — same conditions as your weigh-in, so nothing drifts between reads.
  • 2Tape snug and level — flat against skin, parallel to the floor, not digging in or compressing.
  • 3Measure each spot 2–3× and average — if two reads disagree, take a third.
  • 4Every ~2 weeks, not daily — muscle and fat move slowly; daily reads are just noise.

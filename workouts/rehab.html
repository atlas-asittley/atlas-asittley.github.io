HOW, NOT HOW MANY

  • Slowest speed you can. This is not a set.
  • Smallest range that does NOT provoke. Back off before it bites.
  • Breathe out through it.
  • Do not brace. That is the whole point — go in relaxed.
  • Add range weekly — never load, never speed.
Why these six: they are the movements you actually avoid or brace through, taken from your own function check-ins plus the lower-back bend you reported on 2026-11-19. The idea is to practise the real movements relaxed, rather than do a separate exercise list.

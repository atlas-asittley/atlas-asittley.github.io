Daily Targets — modest lean bulk

~170–180g protein · ~3,400 calories (a slight surplus over ~3,150–3,300 maintenance).

The lever is calorie-dense food, not volume. Let the scale creep up slowly — a little fat is fine. Don't judge by the daily scale (it can't tell muscle-gain-with-fat-loss from a stall): judge by strength + monthly photos + tape (waist / shoulders / arms) on the History page. Weigh as a weekly average for the trend only.

Shake Slot — rotate · ~700–750 cal / ~35–40g P each · NO POWDER (it was the gas trigger)

1. Mass Gainer Shake ~745 cal / ~40g P

Blend the liquids briefly, let it sit so foam collapses. (Protein comes from the milk + PB — no powder.)

  • 2 cups Fairlife lactose-free milk
  • ½ cup dry oats
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 banana

2. Mocha Almond Mass Shake ~700 cal / ~39g P

Blend the liquids & cocoa, let it settle. (No powder — protein from the milk + almond butter.)

  • 2 cups Fairlife lactose-free milk
  • 2 tbsp almond butter
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • ½ cup decaf cold brew or 1 shot decaf espresso (you already get caffeine from morning coffee)
  • ¼ cup dry oats + 2 dates (or 1 tbsp honey)

3. No-Blend PB-Banana Proats ~755 cal / ~28g P

Spoonable, no blender, no froth. Just cook the oats and stir in the rest. Works hot or overnight-cold. (Lower protein than the others — pair with eggs or Greek yogurt if you want more.)

  • ¾ cup rolled oats cooked in 1 cup Fairlife
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 banana (or ½ cup berries)
  • drizzle of honey

Snack Slot — rotate · ~530–590 cal / ~30–43g P each · no powder

1. Greek Yogurt Power Bowl ~575 cal / ~37g P

A big Greek-yogurt base carries the protein (no powder), then top.

  • 1½ cups plain Greek yogurt
  • ¼ cup granola
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • ¼ cup nuts
  • a little vanilla + a little cinnamon
  • optional: berries

2. Savory Cottage Cheese Bowl ~590 cal / ~43g P

Use lactose-free cottage cheese (Green Valley / Lactaid-friendly). No protein powder — protein comes from a bigger cottage-cheese base.

  • 1½ cups lactose-free cottage cheese
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, drizzled
  • ¼ cup nuts or pumpkin seeds
  • black pepper + flaky salt + everything-bagel seasoning
  • mix-ins (rotate): cucumber + cherry tomato · scallion + chili crisp · avocado · furikake · on toast

3. PB-Banana Toast + Milk ~530 cal / ~26g P

Good on the move. (Lowest-protein snack — fine as a calorie top-off.)

  • 2 slices whole-grain or sourdough toast
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 banana, sliced + drizzle of honey
  • 1 cup Fairlife (lactose-free, ~13g protein)

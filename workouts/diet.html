Daily Targets

~180g protein · ~3,000–3,400 calories (slight surplus over ~2,800–3,000 maintenance).

The lever is total calories, not protein — appetite is the bottleneck, so eat calorie-dense, not high-volume. Aim ~0.5 lb/week up. Flat = eat more; >1 lb/week = mostly fat, ease off.

Recipes

1. Mass Gainer Shake ~900 cal / ~100g P

Liquids first, protein powder last, pulse briefly, let it sit 2–3 min so foam collapses.

  • 2 cups Fairlife lactose-free milk
  • ½ cup dry oats
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 banana
  • 1 serving protein powder (~60g P)

This replaces the plain shake (it already has a full protein serving).

2. Greek Yogurt Power Bowl ~560 cal / ~45g P

Stir protein powder into the yogurt first (smooth, no froth), then top.

  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ½ scoop protein powder
  • ¼ cup granola
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • handful of nuts (~1 oz)
  • optional: berries

Daily Targets — modest lean bulk

~170–180g protein · ~3,400 calories (a slight surplus over ~3,150–3,300 maintenance).

The lever is calorie-dense food, not volume. Let the scale creep up slowly — a little fat is fine. Don't judge by the daily scale (it can't tell muscle-gain-with-fat-loss from a stall): judge by strength + monthly photos + tape (waist / shoulders / arms) on the History page. Shake Slot — rotate · ~900 cal / ~100g P each

1. Mass Gainer Shake ~900 cal / ~100g P

Liquids first, protein powder last, pulse briefly, let it sit 2–3 min so foam collapses.

  • 2 cups Fairlife lactose-free milk
  • ½ cup dry oats
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 banana
  • 1 serving protein powder (~60g P)

2. Mocha Almond Mass Shake ~870 cal / ~95g P

Same method — liquids & cocoa first, powder last, pulse, let it settle.

  • 2 cups Fairlife lactose-free milk
  • 1 serving protein powder (~60g P)
  • 2 tbsp almond butter
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • ½ cup decaf cold brew or 1 shot decaf espresso (you already get caffeine from morning coffee)
  • ¼ cup dry oats + 2 dates (or 1 tbsp honey)

3. No-Blend PB-Banana Proats ~855 cal / ~94g P

Spoonable, no blender, no froth. Cook oats, stir protein in off the heat (warm, not boiling, so it doesn't clump). Works hot or overnight-cold.

  • ¾ cup rolled oats cooked in 1 cup Fairlife
  • 1 serving protein powder (~60g P), stirred in off heat
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 banana (or ½ cup berries)
  • drizzle of honey

Snack Slot — rotate · ~560 cal / ~40g P each

1. Greek Yogurt Power Bowl ~560 cal / ~40g P

Stir protein powder into the yogurt first (smooth, no froth), then top.

  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ½ scoop protein powder
  • ¼ cup granola
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • ¼ cup nuts
  • a little vanilla + a little cinnamon
  • optional: berries

2. Savory Cottage Cheese Bowl ~590 cal / ~43g P

Use lactose-free cottage cheese (Green Valley / Lactaid-friendly). No protein powder — protein comes from a bigger cottage-cheese base.

  • 1½ cups lactose-free cottage cheese
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, drizzled
  • ¼ cup nuts or pumpkin seeds
  • black pepper + flaky salt + everything-bagel seasoning
  • mix-ins (rotate): cucumber + cherry tomato · scallion + chili crisp · avocado · furikake · on toast

3. PB-Banana Toast + Protein Milk ~600 cal / ~40g P

No dairy bowl — good on the move.

  • 2 slices whole-grain or sourdough toast
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 banana, sliced + drizzle of honey
  • 1 cup Fairlife with ½ scoop protein shaken in

FAQ

Is it really free, with no subscription?
Yes — every feature, including barcode scanning, no upsell.
Is my data private?
Yes. It's stored only in your browser. No account, no server, no tracking of what you eat.
Does the barcode scanner work on my phone?
It works in browsers with camera barcode support (most Android/Chrome). On any device you can search foods by name.

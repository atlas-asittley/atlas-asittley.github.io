Bo's Backyard: privacy

A private party game run by Drew for his friends. Last updated November 15, 2026.

What we collect

When you ask to join, we keep the email address you give (and a name, if you add one) so Drew can let you in. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us only your email address, to check it is on Drew's guest list. Inside the game we keep what you make there: your character's look, coins and items.

What we do with it

Only to decide who can play and to run the game. Drew gets a message with your email when you ask to join. We don't sell or share it, use it for ads, or email you anything except the one note saying you're in.

Where it lives

Join requests are stored with Supabase; sign-in is handled by Cloudflare (and Google, if you choose it); the game itself and your character are stored on Drew's own computer.

Removing your information

Ask Drew, or email atlas.asittley@gmail.com, and we'll remove your email from the guest list and delete your join request.

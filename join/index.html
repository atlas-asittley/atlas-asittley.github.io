BO'S BACKYARD

Welcome! Let's get you playing

You play with two things:

A screen to watch: a TV, a projector or a laptop, anything with a web browser. Everyone shares it.
Your phone is your controller. Each player uses their own.

Start here on your phone: typing is easier.

First time?

  1. On your phone: ask to join
  2. Wait for Drew to let you in (you'll get an email)
  3. On the screen's web browser, go to drewplays.com and sign in with the same email
  4. Scan the code on the screen with your phone's camera. You're in!

Already let in?

On the screen: open its web browser and go to drewplays.com. Sign in, and the game comes up with a code in the corner.

This is the screen: open the game

On your phone: point your camera at the code on the screen and tap the link. That makes this phone your controller.

Make this phone your controller

Already let in: play

Step 1: your email

Drew gets a message and lets you in. Use the email you'll sign in with (a Gmail address lets you sign in with Google).

Your email is only used to let you into the game.

Step 2: wait for Drew

  1. You asked
  2. Drew lets you in (you get an email)
  3. Sign in on the screen
  4. Scan the screen's code with this phone

When the "you're in" email arrives, go to your screen (a TV, projector or laptop), open its web browser, go to drewplays.com and sign in with the same email.

The game comes up with a code in the corner. Scan it with this phone's camera and sign in once more on the phone. This phone is then your controller.

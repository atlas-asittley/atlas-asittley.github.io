TV Remote

One remote on your phone for the Samsung TV and the Roku. Android only.

Download the app
  1. Tap Download, then open the file.
  2. If Android asks, allow Chrome to install apps, then tap Install. (It may warn that the app is from an unknown developer; that's expected for an app that isn't from the Play Store.)
  3. Open TV Remote at home on the Wi-Fi and tap Find TV and Roku.

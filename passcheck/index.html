🔑 Passphrase Check

Paste what you think your Claude-backup passphrase is. This checks it against the real one without knowing the real one — the check runs entirely in your browser and nothing you type is ever sent anywhere.

👁 show / hide

How it works: the app computes a SHA-256 fingerprint of what you type and compares the first 8 hex characters to the known fingerprint 32c14097 of the real passphrase. SHA-256 is one-way, so this page reveals nothing about the actual passphrase. You can even save this page and run it offline (airplane mode) — it needs no network.
